Neo Digital Inc. ("Neo Digital", "we") operates NEO Pulse and the Neo Agent Hub (the "Service") on Render at neo-agent-hub-web.onrender.com with API services at neo-agent-hub-api.onrender.com. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use the Service.

Information we collect

  • Account identifiers (name, email, team membership) needed to sign in and authorize access.
  • OAuth tokens and integration settings you connect (for example Google or Slack), stored encrypted where configured.
  • Usage and audit data required to operate agents, logs, and security monitoring.
  • Client workspace content you submit through the Service (for example site metadata, prompts, or exports).

How we use information

  • Provide, secure, and improve the Service for Neo Digital and authorized users.
  • Run integrations you enable (Search Console, ads, WordPress, LLM providers, and similar tools).
  • Respond to support requests and enforce our Terms of Service.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share data with subprocessors needed to run the Service (for example Render hosting, Google, Slack, or OpenRouter) under their policies, and when required by law.

Retention and security

We retain data while accounts are active and as needed for legal, backup, or client contractual obligations. We use encryption, access controls, and least-privilege practices appropriate to a production SaaS deployment.

Your choices

You may disconnect integrations in settings where available. For access, correction, or deletion requests, contact us at sean@neodigital.ca.
