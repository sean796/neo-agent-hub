# Meeting recap in Google Drive (one-pager)

**For:** Client and internal stakeholders  
**Product name (working):** Meeting Notes → Drive  
**Delivery:** Slack (Neo Agent Hub) + Google Doc in your Shared Drive

## What you get

After a call, Neo Digital turns the Gemini meeting note into a **clear action doc** in Google Drive: decisions, owners, due dates, and SEO-related follow-ups when they came up in the meeting. Your team still gets the fast **task list and checklist in Slack**; the Drive doc is the durable record you can share with the client or link in your PM tool.

## How it works (client-visible steps)

1. Your Neo Digital lead runs `/meeting-notes` in Slack and picks the right Gemini note (or uses latest).
2. Slack shows the structured task list and checklist for the team.
3. One click **Publish to Drive** creates or updates a Doc from Neo Digital’s template (client name and date in the title).
4. Slack returns a **link to the Doc** (only people with Drive access can open it).

## What we need from you

- Google Workspace access for the teammate who connects Google (one-time consent).
- A Shared Drive folder or template Doc we can copy (set up once per client or agency-wide).

## What this is not

- Not a replacement for your CRM or contract scope.
- Not automatic client email; you choose when to share the Doc link.
- Not legal advice; the doc reflects what was discussed in the meeting note.

## Outcome

Less time writing recaps, fewer “what did we agree on?” threads, and one place in Drive that matches what the team already saw in Slack.
