# Client SEO pulse in Slack (one-pager)

**For:** Retainer clients with a shared Slack channel  
**Product name (working):** SEO Pulse  
**Delivery:** Scheduled or on-demand post in `#client-yourbrand` (or internal `#int-yourbrand`)

## What you get

A **regular SEO snapshot** in Slack: how search visibility moved versus last period, notable new queries, and **one prioritized recommendation** with a named owner on the Neo Digital side. No need to open Search Console or a dashboard for the weekly check-in.

## What the post includes

- Clicks and impressions trend (month over month or week over week, as configured).
- A short list of queries worth attention (gains, losses, or new).
- One **recommended next action** (for example: refresh a page, fix a coverage issue, expand a topic cluster).
- Optional link to a **Client SEO Log** Google Doc or Sheet when you want a running history.

## How it works

- Neo Digital connects your site in our internal Pulse workspace (Search Console via authorized access you already provide for SEO work).
- Neo Agent Hub posts the digest on a schedule you agree (for example Monday 8:00 AM Mountain) or when your lead runs `/seo-pulse`.
- Clients in the channel see the same post if the channel is Slack Connect; otherwise we mirror a summary in your client-facing channel.

## What we need from you

- Continued Search Console property access for your domain (existing retainer setup).
- A Slack channel for pulse posts (shared or internal-only, your choice).
- A default @mention or owner name for the “recommended action” line.

## What this is not

- Not a guarantee of rankings or traffic.
- Not real-time alerting for every crawl error (deeper audits stay scoped separately).
- Not a substitute for monthly strategy calls; it **feeds** those calls with fresh numbers.

## Outcome

Clients feel continuous oversight; your team spends less time assembling “what changed in GSC?” slides before every touchpoint.
