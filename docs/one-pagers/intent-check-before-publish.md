# Intent check before publish (one-pager)

**For:** Content and SEO leads (internal first; optional client sign-off PDF later)  
**Product name (working):** Intent Check  
**Delivery:** Slack `/intent-check` + optional Google Drive export

## What you get

Before a page or post goes live, Neo Digital runs an **intent match review**: does the draft align with what search results and SERP features expect for the target query? You get a **match score**, missing entities or sections, suggested H2s, and FAQ ideas, grounded in **live SERP data**, not guesswork.

## How it works

1. In Slack, run `/intent-check` with a target keyword and either a **URL**, a **Google Doc link**, or pasted outline text.
2. Our server fetches SERP context (DataForSEO or Semrush, same stack as Pulse research).
3. OpenRouter compares SERP intent to your draft and returns structured feedback in Slack.
4. Optional: export a one-page summary to Drive for client approval on high-stakes pages.

## What we need from you

- Target query and draft content (link or paste).
- For Doc links: Google connect on the teammate running the check (read-only Docs scope when that ships).

## What this is not

- Not auto-rewriting or publishing content without human review.
- Not a replacement for brand, legal, or medical compliance review.
- Not a promise of ranking position; it reduces **intent mismatch** before ship.

## Outcome

Fewer post-launch rewrites, stronger AISEO positioning (“we QA for search intent, not just keywords”), and a repeatable gate before WordPress or bulk generator runs in Pulse.
